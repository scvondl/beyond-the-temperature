THE RESEARCH QUESTION

What does it mean for a park to perform better?

Temperature alone cannot describe how people experience public space. Shade, movement, activities and individual perceptions complicate any simple ranking of parks.

We move from citywide maps to park reviews and visitors’ own accounts of heat, shade and comfort.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLE Measured conditions ≠ lived comfort. We are testing a question, not claiming a causal relationship.
01 / MEASUREWhat is the environment like?

How to read the landscape map

Try NDVI on side A and NDBI on side B. Drag the divider in Swipe mode to compare the same place.

NDVI · island greenness
Shows where satellites detect green vegetation. Darker green means a stronger vegetation signal.
NDBI · built-surface signal
Shows where built surfaces or bare ground stand out. Darker brown means a stronger signal.
VBS · 0–100 display
The Vegetation–Built Score combines greenness and the built-surface signal. Greener areas with a weaker built-surface signal score higher and appear darker green.

ABOUT THE DATA

What we know.
What we don't.

01 · Air temperature

Historical air-temperature references (2021–2024) come from station records assigned to planning areas, not measurements inside every park. The live NEA layer checks for updated station readings every five minutes while open, showing observation times and labelling any saved fallback.

02 · Spatial context

Land surface temperature (LST; 2021–2025, 150 m) describes surface heat, while NDVI, NDBI and VBS (2021–2025, 20 m) describe vegetation and built-surface signals. The atlases share 48 matched park boundaries, with 45 heat scores and 37 VBS scores. These indicators do not directly measure shade or comfort.

03 · Public reviews

The 54-park study contains 52,419 collected review entries, including 24,189 with written text across 53 parks. The remaining listed park has no reviews. These self-selected, unevenly distributed comments reveal what some visitors mention, not representative measures of thermal comfort.

04 · Surveys & observations

Map 04 loads published survey responses with mapped locations from Ushahidi. The illustrations group accounts by the setting visitors describe, such as a shaded path or an open lawn. The map checks for updates while it is open.
