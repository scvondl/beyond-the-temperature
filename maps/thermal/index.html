DATA & METHODS

Reading this atlas

LST

Surface temperature · 2021–2025

The supplied U5K interim composite and five annual rasters, at 150 m. The web display uses nearest-neighbour reprojection with transparent NoData; it does not fill observation gaps. Colours share a fixed 26–47°C scale. View the source observation-year counts by clicking a composite pixel.

AIR

Background air · 2021–2024

Calendar-month-balanced historical station means assigned to planning areas. Areas without an eligible local station use the documented nearest-station proxy. This is a background reference, not a continuous measured air-temperature surface.

0–100

Park heat score · 45 supported parks

100 × [0.5 × normalized park LST + 0.5 × normalized background air]. Scores are copied unchanged from the supplied result, using its fixed 45-park reference cohort. Higher means relatively hotter. The two input periods differ. Unsupported values remain unavailable.

LIVE

Latest station air readings

NEA station observations from the public data.gov.sg API, refreshed every five minutes while the layer is open. The actual observation time is always shown. A labelled saved snapshot may be used if the API is unavailable; readings are never interpolated or used to recalculate historical scores.

Surface temperature and air temperature measure different things. Their map legends use different ranges. Park scores are relative indicators; they do not measure thermal comfort or prove a cooling effect. Annual maps may also differ in seasonal observation support.
